MK IPTV · by Mkoussa

Delete your MK IPTV sync account and data

MK IPTV only creates an account if you turn on account sync (Sign in with Google, Sign in with Apple, or a link code). You can delete it at any time.

Option 1 – in the app (immediate)

  1. Open MK IPTV and go to Settings › Sync.
  2. Tap Delete synced data and confirm.

Option 2 – by email

Email mohamad@koussa.org with the subject “MK IPTV account deletion”, from the email address you used with Sign in with Google/Apple (or include the link code shown in the app). We delete the account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sync account (Firebase user ID and the email address from Google/Apple).
  • All synced data: playlists (server, username, password), profiles, favourites, watch progress, settings and linked-device names.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept in the cloud after deletion; there is no extra retention period.
  • Data stored on your own devices stays there until you remove it in the app or uninstall the app.
